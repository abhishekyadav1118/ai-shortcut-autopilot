# Music Asset Licenses and Log

Place royalty-free, copyright-safe background music audio files (`.mp3` or `.wav`) in this directory (`assets/music/`).
The rendering engine randomly selects one track for each video run, adjusts volume to `-24dB`, ducks under narration, and normalizes to `-14 LUFS`.
